How we help communities

  • ✓ We educate schools and families on sustainable habits that reduce waste and preserve resources.
  • ✓ We partner with local leaders to run recycling and cleanup campaigns that improve neighborhoods.

What you can do

  • ✓ Sort recyclables and reduce single-use plastic whenever possible.
  • ✓ Fix leaks, use water wisely, and save electricity at home.

  • ✓ Plant native trees and support green spaces in your area.
